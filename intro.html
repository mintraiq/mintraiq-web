Questions

Before you start.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You upload statements you download from your bank's website, or photograph receipts. MintrAIQ never asks for your online banking login.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Insight Starter is free: a 3-month dashboard, 3 statement uploads and 3 receipt scans a month. Cashflow Essential and Forecast Pro add more.

Can I use it on my computer?

Yes. Sign in on the web with the same account you use on your phone.

Which AI services see my data?

Google and Groq. Our Privacy Policy has the detail.
